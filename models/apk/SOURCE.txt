Owner-supplied original acoustic guitar and hand animations

Source: apk_extracted-20261002T162048Z-1-001.zip, supplied by the project
owner as their Android application. Converted locally for review at the
owner's request. No redistribution licence is inferred from the archive.
Publishing these assets remains subject to the owner's deployment approval.

Content: Acoustic_Mesh, AcousticTexture, AcousticRozet, CharNewMesh,
SolKolHighDiffuseMap, SolKolHighBumpMap_NRM1, and the 118 original playing
AnimationClips. App code, native binaries, UI, audio, and other guitars
are not included.

Conversion preserves the original geometry, UVs, skin bindings and cubic
animation tangents. Unity coordinates are reflected for glTF; the scene
is uniformly scaled and reoriented to the player. Arms are cropped to
32 mm behind each wrist and faded; their remaining influences attach to
the wrist. The original pick and six string meshes remain separate.

The 96 chord poses and 22 strumming/picking clips retain their source names.
Chord shapes were checked against the supplied ChordDB and posed geometry;
the simple open Em7 pose was also identified from that geometry. Measured
string crossings retime the two played strokes to the existing audio clock.
No source audio or Android application code is executed by the web app.

hand-poses.json is a locally generated adaptation, not an extracted source
animation. It covers the web app's offered shapes on the original skeleton
without replacing or rescaling the mesh. The GLB SHA-256 binds it to this rig.
Revision 4 checks designated fingertips, approach direction, every sounding
string's vibrating span, and the complete skin against the solid guitar.
It checks front/back sidedness, all vertices, triangle centroids and edge
midpoints, including the thumb, unused fingers and fading wrist. It uses
an authored wrist basis without changing the contact or joint limits.
The whole fretting thumb stays behind the neck even beyond its side edges,
not merely where its skin projects over the guitar. Its distal pad supports
the rear neck within 0.12 stage units at each settled pose. Transitions may
release contact but retain the rear-side constraint on every update.
Native knuckle roll and thumb opposition are included rather than reduced
to spread and flexion alone. Each pose stores a wrist transform followed
by five values per digit: spread, two flexions, distal flexion and roll.
The bank includes 3,798 offered shapes, 34 additional original preview
shapes and a resting hand. Run npm run prepare:apk-hands to regenerate it;
the full geometric coverage checks must pass before the catalogue is
written. Bounded transition corrections are checked before display, with
a clear previous/partial pose retained if the proposed step is obstructed.
Source chord previews use the corresponding corrected shape; the original
strumming/picking motions and the raw GLB curves remain unchanged.

The existing WebXR reference hands and their separate MIT licence are
unchanged. This asset does not inherit that licence.
